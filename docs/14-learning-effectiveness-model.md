# 14 — Learning Effectiveness Model

## Learning loop

Patternly uses retrieval, immediate diagnostic feedback where appropriate, spaced review, contrast, and deliberate repetition. A correct answer alone is not proof of understanding; performance is interpreted with the amount and stage of evidence behind it.

The evidence model separates `evidenceVolume`, `learningStageEvidence`, and `performanceSignals`. It does not collect confidence or produce readiness, retention, or mastery percentages. A visible metric needs enough evidence, must answer a concrete training question, and must lead to an action.

## Feedback

`Reason` gives concise immediate orientation. `Details` is collapsed but complete and available after correct, partial, or incorrect work. It explains mechanism and application, corrects the selected error, and supplies transfer when useful. The learner may open Details without changing domain state. Hints exist only in designed interactions and only their actual use can create review evidence.

## Review and progression

Review binds a source item to skill, competency, or taxonomy evidence. It may grow from incorrect/partial work, supported hint use, wrong pattern/strategy, complexity error, repeated mistake, scheduled retrieval, weak taxonomy evidence, or manual marking. Persistent review resolves only after two successful after-due attempts; a same-session correction does not resolve it.

Algorithms uses exactly `Learn Approach`, `Guided Practice`, `Recognize Patterns`, `Contrast Practice`, `Weak Area Review`, `Independent Practice`, and `Interview Simulation`. Review takes due or session-miss source items, then compatible reviewed items, otherwise shortens and shows the actual length. Reinsert is restricted to Guided Practice and both review sources.

Certification uses `Diagnostic Baseline`, `Focus Practice`, `Scenario Practice`, `Weak Area Review`, `Mixed Practice`, `Quick Review`, and `Exam Simulation`. Its remediation batches by competency then topic.

Progression changes recommendations and default focus, never access. Recommendation is deterministic, family-specific, explained, and overridden by the learner's current-session choice. Home prioritizes overdue review and repeated mistakes.
