# 07 — Content Guidelines

## Editorial contract

Content teaches a decision, not recognition of a remembered answer. It is original, factually checked, lawful to use, tied to a stable taxonomy, and human-reviewed before it enters an active bank.

Every instructional item supplies:

```txt
prompt and constraints
→ stable options or interaction elements
→ accepted answer contract
→ concise Reason
→ complete Details
→ taxonomy / skill references and source metadata
```

`Reason` gives immediate decisive orientation. `Details`, collapsed by default and available for correct, partial, and incorrect attempts, forms one coherent narrative: mechanism and application; correction of the selected error; then transfer rule or counterexample when useful. Expanding Details never changes scoring, review, or other domain state.

Every active wrong option in an instructional choice item has a meaningful authored explanation under its stable option ID. Runtime combines it with the authored Details after the learner selects that option; it does not fabricate educational copy. Improve or remove any distractor that cannot be explained. Ordering and complexity items explain the actual relation or dimension error.

## Algorithms content

One batch addresses one mental unit. Editorial order is active roadmap units, highest false-heuristic risk, contrasts and mistake diagnosis, then remaining foundations and mechanics. Content may train recognition, strategy, ordering, complexity, and independent reasoning, but does not simulate a full external coding platform.

Ordering items contain at least two elements and support adjacent-relation scoring. Complexity items explicitly declare checked dimensions, available values, accepted values or aliases; time-only and space-only are valid.

## Certification content

Certification items use stable official sources. Items that depend on volatile price or UI data include their source and version in their contract. Remediation is batched by competency area and then topic. Content does not copy exam dumps or imply affiliation.

## Batch rubric

Human sign-off verifies factual correctness; prompt/options/correct-answer consistency; a decisive Reason; mechanism and application in Details; every distractor explanation; transfer; constraints and preconditions; correct complexity derivation; absence of false heuristics; alignment to the primary skill atom; no unnecessary solution dump; and source legality.

## Content correction

Correct active content in place. Do not retain obsolete explanation versions, map earlier item explanations, hide weak content, assign a temporary quality status, or use a compatibility contract for local history. A content version names the active content bank only.

Codex may assist with bounded editorial work but cannot invent an unreviewed mass rewrite or claim human sign-off. Missing content is an explicit error, never a generic replacement.
