# 06 — Branding and Style Direction

## Brand promise

Patternly is precise, focused practice: identify the decision, understand the mechanism, and take the next useful action. The tone is calm, direct, and technically credible—not gamified, anxious, or officially authoritative.

## Copy rules

Prefer: “Focus on interval boundaries”, “Review due: repeated strategy error”, “Practice the next contrast”, “Evidence is still limited”, and “This simulation follows the referenced exam profile.”

Avoid claims of readiness, retention, mastery, certainty, guaranteed results, official exam status, affiliation, or AI recommendation. State the evidence and recommended action instead.

Use `Reason` for immediate orientation and `Details` for the complete instructional explanation. Errors are useful signals: say that content is unavailable, a profile is unresolved, or a session cannot resume rather than substituting generic copy.

## Certification safety

Certification names and trademarks identify subject matter only. Patternly must state that it is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by an exam provider. An internal practice threshold, if present, is explicitly Patternly-defined and never looks like an official pass/fail result.

## Visual direction

Use a focused workbench rather than a game board: restrained surfaces, high contrast, compact hierarchy, stable track accents, and unambiguous response states. The visual system supports concentrated practice and accessibility; it does not turn calculable metrics into status theatre.
