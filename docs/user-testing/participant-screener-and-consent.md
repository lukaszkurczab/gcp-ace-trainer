# Participant screener and consent/data note

## Recruitment target

Recruit 6–8 adults for one Algorithms cohort. The intended participant:

- is at least 18;
- expects to prepare for a software-engineering interview in the next six
  months;
- can work with technical English;
- can solve basic array or hash-map exercises;
- sometimes struggles to recognize an applicable pattern, explain a trade-off,
  or handle a boundary condition;
- has not contributed to Patternly and has not previously seen the tested
  content.

Do not recruit a GCP ACE cohort under this protocol. The current pinned bank was
reviewed against the official guide, but GCP requires its own cohort decision,
participant task, representative sample, moderator script and acceptance gate.

## Minimal screener

The recruiter records only a participant code and the answers below in the
research folder. Contact details used for scheduling stay in the recruiter's
separate scheduling system.

1. Are you 18 or older?
   Eligible: yes.
2. Are you preparing, or likely to prepare, for a software-engineering
   interview in the next six months?
   Eligible: yes.
3. Which statement best describes your current practice?
   - I have not yet solved basic array or hash-map tasks.
   - I can solve some basic tasks, but choosing a pattern, explaining a
     trade-off, or handling boundaries is inconsistent.
   - I reliably solve and explain unfamiliar medium/hard interview tasks.
   Target: the middle answer. The last answer may be retained only to avoid an
   artificially uniform sample; record the exception.
4. Can you read technical questions and feedback in English without live
   translation?
   Eligible: yes. The moderator may speak Polish, but must not translate or
   simplify learning content during the task.
5. Have you worked on Patternly, reviewed its question bank, or taken part in a
   previous Patternly session?
   Eligible: no.
6. Which do you currently use when preparing? Select any: coding challenge
   platform, notes/flashcards, course/video, mock interview, AI assistant,
   tutor/peer, none.
   This is context, not an eligibility rule.
7. Which phone platform can you comfortably use for the session: iOS, Android,
   either?
   Use only for session logistics.
8. Are you willing to take part in a 45–60 minute moderated product-research
   session?
   Eligible: yes.

Do not collect employer, exact job title, home address, date of birth,
government identifier, salary, health information, or interview-company names.
If a participant volunteers sensitive information, do not copy it into notes.

## Confirmation message

Before the session, send:

> You will try an English-language learning app while a moderator observes how
> understandable and useful it is. This is a test of the product, not a test of
> you. The session lasts 45–60 minutes. You may skip a question or stop at any
> time. We will not ask for passwords, employer information, or confidential
> interview material.

The research owner sends confirmed logistics, compensation, contact, and the
applicable privacy notice with this message. Recruitment must not start while
any of those operational details is unknown; a moderator must not guess them.

## Start-of-session consent

Read this in the chosen moderation language before opening the app.

### English

> We are evaluating Patternly, not your ability. I will ask you to use the app
> and describe what you expect. You can decline any question or stop at any
> time. We will use a participant code in research notes and will not put your
> name or contact details in those notes. Your decision will not affect
> compensation already agreed for this session.
>
> Do you consent to participate?

### Polish

> Oceniamy aplikację Patternly, a nie Twoje umiejętności. Poproszę Cię o
> korzystanie z aplikacji i opisywanie swoich oczekiwań. Możesz pominąć dowolne
> pytanie albo przerwać udział w każdej chwili. W notatkach użyjemy kodu
> uczestnika; nie wpiszemy do nich imienia ani danych kontaktowych. Twoja
> decyzja nie wpłynie na wcześniej uzgodnione wynagrodzenie za tę sesję.
>
> Czy zgadzasz się na udział?

Record the yes/no participation answer. Stop if participation consent is
absent. The first cohort is notes-only under PO-013 A: do not request or make
screen, audio or video recordings.

## Data handling

- Use a study-generated participant code in every artifact.
- Keep recruitment contact data outside research notes.
- Store written notes only in the access-controlled location named by the
  research owner.
- For the first solo cohort, the product owner is the named moderator,
  note-taker, and synthesis owner. Access is limited to that person unless this
  protocol is updated before recruitment.
- Delete scheduling contact data after the last required logistics message.
- Delete participant-level notes within 90 days after the synthesis decision
  and no later than 180 days after the session; the de-identified
  cross-participant synthesis may be retained with product decisions.
- Record the actual deletion dates in the study log.
- If a participant withdraws before synthesis, delete their participant-level
  notes and exclude the case.
- Do not upload raw notes to an unapproved transcription or AI service.

The research owner must confirm that these periods and the storage location
match the applicable privacy notice before recruitment. If they do not, the
owner replaces them with the approved periods in this canonical protocol and
records the change.
