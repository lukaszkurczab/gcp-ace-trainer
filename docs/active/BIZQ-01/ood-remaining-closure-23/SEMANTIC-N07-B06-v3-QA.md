# Independent semantic review — OOD-N07-B06 v3

**Verdict: REVISE.** This report is bound to the frozen whole-object input hash below. It covers all 18 items, not a sampled subset. The input’s mechanical 18/90 check verifies shape and scoring only; it does not establish semantic acceptance.

All 18 questions ask the learner to separate external representation from domain meaning. The cases are generally concrete and the keyed mapping is supported by the first scenario fact, but every second constraint tells the learner to put mapping/normalization at the adapter and pass domain facts; the Reason then repeats it. Many wrong-option messages reuse owner/fact taxonomy and some are overbroad for their target. B06-i007 additionally lacks enough timestamp information to determine an instant for an ambiguous local time.

## Cohort evidence

The second constraint in all 18 items is an answer recipe; the Reason repeats that recipe in its second sentence and scenarioApplication repeats it again. This violates the pre-answer disclosure and explanation distinctions in BIZQ-01 §4.2/§4.4 and canonical content guidance. The observed longest-key count (17/18 uniquely longest for this unit) corroborates the systematic shape but is not a numeric threshold or length-parity rule. A repair should retain decisive neutral case facts and make real competing policies plausible; it should not pad options or mechanically equalize lengths.

B06-i007 also needs a timestamp disambiguation premise or a different question: a local time plus a route time zone can still be ambiguous during a repeated daylight-saving interval. RFC 3339 and Python datetime documentation support this general distinction.
