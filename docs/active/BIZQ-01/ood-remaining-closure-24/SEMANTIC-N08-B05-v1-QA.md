## Blocking findings

### F1 — Correct-option form cue and weak distractor comparability

Section 4.3 requires plausible alternatives at comparable specificity and calls for removing a systematic longest-correct signal; it does not impose equal word counts. Here 17 of 18 accepted options are strictly longer than every distractor. The repeated content pattern matters: the key typically states the complete item-scoped atomic operation, while alternatives often directly repeat separate safe calls despite the prompt saying those are individually safe, propose a catalog-wide lock despite an explicit independence requirement, or defer repair until after readers observe a bad state. For example, i006 key length is 87 characters versus 71 for the longest distractor; i016 is 150 versus 99. Most items still teach useful variants, but the near-uniform complete-key/partial-or-extreme-wrong pattern makes form and elimination do too much work. Revise choices to provide credible, complete competing policies that fail one visible condition; do not pad choices or target a length/count. This is an editorial-quality defect under the existing criterion, not an automatic rejection by metric.

### F2 — i011 composite-key and conflicting-duplicate rule do not fit together

The visible prompt says the completion key contains learner, exercise revision, and score policy, then says a resubmission for the same revision must not replace the policy used by the accepted score. The key says to use the complete composite identity and also reject a conflicting duplicate revision. If score policy is part of the identity, the same learner and exercise revision with a different policy is a different composite key, so the key does not itself detect the conflict it says to reject. If duplicate detection instead uses learner plus exercise revision, that identity rule is not stated and the Details “complete composite identity” does not distinguish which fields belong in the key. Clarify the identity boundary so one action follows from the prompt; e.g. separate a stable completion identity (learner + exercise revision) from its immutable stored policy value, or state an alternate visible duplicate rule. This is a one-best-answer/contract clarity issue under §§4.1–4.2, not a new guarantee.

No other item-level key or diagnostic defect was found. Reason fields generally state the scenario condition and selected atomicity boundary. The repeated transfer sentence in Details is generic, but is not a separate blocker: the case-specific mechanism/scenario/error-correction fields carry the explanation. Carried references do not establish fictional application behavior; conclusions here rely on the explicitly given scenario facts.
