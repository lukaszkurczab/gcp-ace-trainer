## Blocking finding — answer position and form disclose the key

BIZQ-01 §4.2 requires checking learner-visible answer disclosure and §4.3 requires removing a systematic longest-correct cue. Every accepted option is first in its array and strictly longer than the distractors (18/18). The current application preserves that order from canonicalQuestionViewModel through CertificationPracticeSessionScreen to PracticeResponseControls, which renders the incoming array in order. The correct answer is therefore visibly first for every question. The content repeats a complete “same ID, recorded outcome, distinct new ID remains allowed” prescription, contrasted with fresh-ID resubmission, overbroad permanent dedupe, or treating a timeout as proof of rejection. The length signal is evidence of the repeated form cue, not a count threshold; e.g. i001 is 133 characters versus a 99-character longest distractor, and i011 is 139 versus 77. Resolve the direct index cue through the existing content/presentation owner and keep alternatives credible and scoped to the visible retry identity. Do not impose an equal-length or option-count rule or a new randomization policy.
