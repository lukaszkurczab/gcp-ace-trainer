## Blocking finding — systematic form and answer-position disclosure

BIZQ-01 §4.3 says to remove a systematic longest-correct signal; §4.2 requires checking learner-visible answer disclosure. Every item has the accepted option strictly longest (18/18), and the key content is a complete multi-clause prescription while distractors usually state an obvious direct violation: cancel shared work for one caller, publish before validation, lie about a timeout/commit, leak a temporary file, or globally cancel unrelated work. For example, i001 is 167 characters versus 112 for its longest distractor; i004 is 175 versus 113; i008 is 171 versus 132. The cue is the repeated content structure, not a threshold or requirement for equal lengths. Revise the choices so the learner must distinguish credible lifecycle contracts using the stated ownership and commit facts; do not pad distractors or target a word count.
